## Purpose
The routing plane joins catalog facts, operator policy, request demand, live quota, failure backoff, effective cost, and observed speed to select a reproducible provider/model route. It records every decision so later explanation can show the selected route, rejected candidates, provenance, objective, and outcome. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/model_router.rs:409-538`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/model_router.rs:557-631`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-module/src/main.rs:1918-1920`)

## Vocabulary
Route key: the canonical provider and model identity; Fusiform and the store key facts by provider plus model and never by a bare model id. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-store/tests/route_state_store.rs:2344-2383`)

Catalog fact: existence, token limits, capabilities, and output price synchronized from Fusiform, subject to a same-field operator override. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-module/src/fusiform_catalog.rs:3-12`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/route_state_store.rs:32-39`)

Score provenance: `Curated`, `Override`, `AaInferred`, or `Unscored`; unscored facts are known but not routable. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/model_facts.rs:178-204`)

Demand: IQ and EQ numbers must be within 0 through 100; the demand-bar calculation clamps its input to that same scale. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/routing_config.rs:48-62`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/model_router.rs:655-667`)

Demand bar: `top_score * (demand / 100)^0.35`; a model's fit on each demanded axis is capped at 1.0. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/routing_config.rs:13-13`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/routing_config.rs:48-62`)

Adequacy: the demand-weighted Elo and EQ fit after confidence penalty. Normal routing requires 0.90; best effort may run from 0.60 to 0.90 and emits `belowFloor`. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/route_scoring.rs:58-117`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/routing_config.rs:71-79`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/model_router.rs:727-776`)

Static admission: catalog, exclusion, availability, capability, context, and fast-variant gates applied before transient quota and cooldown state. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/model_router.rs:409-538`)

Effective price: output list price divided by a positive finite subscription multiplier; unknown price normalizes to maximum cost rather than zero. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/model_router.rs:311-340`)

Selection cost: normalized effective price plus quota pressure weighted by 0.5. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/model_router.rs:557-631`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/routing_config.rs:84-86`)

Effectively-free bucket: routes at or below selection cost 0.05 compare adequacy before speed and ignore smaller cost differences. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/routing_config.rs:82-82`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/model_router.rs:831-848`)

Quota wall: a route or account exclusion caused by explicit exhaustion, utilization at or above 0.99, or a reported spendable non-positive pool. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/quota.rs:249-306`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/quota.rs:114-125`)

Failure backoff: route-local cooldown whose default threshold is three consecutive route-attributable terminal failures; it begins at ten minutes and doubles to a six-hour cap. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/failure_backoff.rs:8-23`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/failure_backoff.rs:132-182`)

Routing decision: the durable selected route, request, candidate explanations, policy provenance, and later outcome addressed by decision id or send id. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-store/src/lib.rs:485-537`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-module/src/main.rs:3166-3282`)

## Surfaces
- Tools (agent-facing): routing is automatic for supported worker launches; the TypeScript consumer forwards model-fact, selection, policy, outcome, usage, cooldown, and backoff calls to the routing module. (`packages/core/src/features/model-routing/routing-consumer.ts:110-242`, `packages/core/src/features/model-routing/agent-route-defaults.ts:1-7`)
- Wire operations (subc): Mutate operations are `route.select`, `route.model_upsert`, `route.model_seed_aa`, `route.select_panel`, `route.set_decision_outcome`, `route.record_usage`, `route.record_cooldown`, and `route.clear_failure_backoff`. Query operations are `route.resolve_policy`, `route.model_fact`, `route.model_facts`, `route.list_inferred`, `route.list_unscored`, `route.cooldown_status`, `route.explain`, `route.decisions_list`, and `route.quota_status`. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-module/src/main.rs:3687-3784`)
- `route.select` accepts requirements, available models, exclusions, capabilities, subscription and fast preferences, routing policy, and launch correlation; it returns a selected route with objective and decision id or a typed failure. (`packages/core/src/features/model-routing/routing-consumer.ts:49-73`, `packages/core/src/features/model-routing/routing-consumer.ts:141-162`)
- `route.resolve_policy` merges curated, operator, and caller subscription facts and exclusions before selection. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/launch_policy.rs:140-196`, `packages/core/src/features/model-routing/subscription-classifier.ts:15-45`)
- `route.set_decision_outcome` records a terminal or non-terminal outcome and advances failure-backoff state; `route.clear_failure_backoff` explicitly removes only route-local terminal-failure backoff. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-module/src/main.rs:2924-2992`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-module/src/main.rs:3751-3762`)
- `route.record_usage` persists token and duration observations used to replace catalog speed after enough recent samples. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-store/src/lib.rs:2008-2020`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-store/src/lib.rs:2099-2136`)
- `route.explain` requires exactly one `decisionID` or `sendID` and returns the recorded decision, rejections, quota snapshot, catalog and policy identities, provenance, and outcome without rescoring. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-module/src/main.rs:3193-3282`)
- `route.decisions_list` filters retained decisions by send id, target agent, route key, and inclusive time bounds with a limit from 1 through 100. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-module/src/main.rs:3324-3387`)
- Fusiform surface: the consumer calls `catalog.get` and `catalog.status` on module `fusiform`; a durable cursor stores catalog version and resolve time. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-module/src/fusiform_catalog.rs:33-35`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-store/src/lib.rs:388-392`)
- AA surface: `route.model_seed_aa` ingests a mapped Artificial Analysis index; `route.list_inferred` and `route.list_unscored` expose inferred and awaiting-placement rows. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-module/src/main.rs:106-110`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-module/src/aa_ingest.rs:27-110`)
- Configuration: model facts come from `~/.config/cortexkit/alfonso-routing.jsonc`, while operator exclusions, subscriptions, and relaxed fast preference come from `~/.config/cortexkit/alfonso.jsonc`; malformed operator edits retain the last valid cached policy. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-module/src/config_overrides.rs:1-12`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-module/src/config_overrides.rs:29-33`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-module/src/config_overrides.rs:756-821`)
- Configuration: provider/model specs accept provider or provider/model inclusion and the same forms prefixed by `-` for exclusion. Operator and caller exclusions are checked independently, so caller negation cannot reopen an operator fence. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/provider_model_spec.rs:17-100`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/provider_model_spec.rs:137-176`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/provider_model_spec.rs:252-289`)
- Configuration: `subscription_multiplier` must be finite and greater than zero; AA uses a vault `api_key_ref`, explicit route-key map, and default 14-day review period, while inline API keys are refused. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-module/src/config_overrides.rs:108-183`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-module/src/config_overrides.rs:1234-1360`)
- Panel routing: `route.select_panel` supports `roster` and automatic `n_pick`. Roster pins bypass exclusions and IQ floor but retain availability, quota, cooldown, and capability gates; n-pick may enforce labeled-family diversity, temperature spread, and typed shortfall. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/panel.rs:19-32`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/panel.rs:263-331`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/panel.rs:392-505`)
- Seat routing: an exact provider/model pin has precedence and fails if unavailable or below the 0.90 floor; otherwise ordered model, provider, and family recommendations are considered with known-family diversity. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/seat_routing.rs:222-327`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/seat_routing.rs:329-435`)

## State
- The route state store applies 34 SQLite migrations for model facts and overrides, catalog and AA state, quota and failure state, usage observations, decisions, rejections, and outcomes. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-store/src/lib.rs:143-282`)
- Model facts distinguish seed, override, AA-inferred, and unscored sources; seed and override rows block AA inference from replacing their scores. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/route_state_store.rs:22-74`)
- Fusiform may update existence, rates, limits, and capabilities while preserving score fields and same-field operator overrides. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-store/src/lib.rs:815-845`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-store/tests/route_state_store.rs:2271-2332`)
- Observed speed uses at least 50 output tokens, five samples, up to twenty samples, and a trailing seven-day window; its query uses `model_route_usage_route_observed_idx`. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/route_state_store.rs:89-92`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-store/src/lib.rs:22-24`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-store/src/lib.rs:125-134`)
- Quota cache entries are isolated by provider and account; one exhausted account does not wall a provider while a sibling account remains usable. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/quota.rs:366-373`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-core/tests/quota_overlay.rs:450-505`)
- Routing decisions are retained for 30 days and expired rows are pruned by the module through `delete_decisions_older_than`. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-module/src/main.rs:121-123`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-module/src/main.rs:355-360`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-store/src/lib.rs:1997-2006`)

## Lifecycle
1. Fusiform synchronization decodes served catalog rates as priced, stated-zero, unpriced, or refused; non-USD, negative, and excessive-exponent prices become unknown maximum cost rather than crashing selection. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-module/src/fusiform_catalog.rs:126-219`)
2. First-sight models under operator-known providers enter the store as unscored and cannot route until an operator override, curated seed, or AA inference supplies score placement. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-store/src/lib.rs:830-845`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/model_facts.rs:202-204`)
3. AA maps an exact route key to an index, interpolates the embedded Elo curve, applies a 30 percent floor-gap penalty, inherits EQ from the closest curated neighbor, and uses default speed 100. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/aa_infer.rs:21-54`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/aa_infer.rs:103-169`)
4. Policy resolution combines curated and authenticated subscription providers, operator rules, and caller rules. Operator factual overrides and exclusions remain authoritative. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/launch_policy.rs:140-196`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/provider_model_spec.rs:137-176`)
5. Selection validates IQ and EQ, rejects statically inadmissible routes, overlays cooldown, quota, and failure backoff, and computes the top routable quality frontier without transient walls. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/model_router.rs:409-538`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/model_router.rs:655-683`)
6. The scorer refuses below 0.60, uses best effort from 0.60 through 0.90, and normally shortlists only routes at or above 0.90. Cheapness and speed cannot pull a sub-bar model into the shortlist. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/model_router.rs:727-776`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-core/tests/model_router.rs:207-210`)
7. Within the shortlist, the scorer compares prompt-cache preference and selection cost; two effectively-free routes compare adequacy, then normalized speed from 50 to 200 tokens per second. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/model_router.rs:831-848`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/model_router.rs:541-543`)
8. Quota merging walls a route at explicit exhaustion or 99 percent use. Missing reset time uses a six-hour reprobe horizon; if every candidate is exhausted, break-glass probes the earliest reset across route and capacity scopes. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/quota.rs:249-306`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/break_glass.rs:72-130`)
9. Panel selection shares route admission, collapses requested family diversity, and reports shortfall rather than fabricating unlabeled diversity. Seat selection evaluates a model pin first and then recommendation tiers. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/panel.rs:289-315`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/panel.rs:392-505`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/seat_routing.rs:250-327`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/seat_routing.rs:329-435`)
10. The module records the chosen decision and every rejected candidate before returning; later outcome reports update the record and failure streak. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-module/src/main.rs:2684-2772`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-store/src/lib.rs:1704-1749`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-module/src/main.rs:2924-2992`)
11. Usage reports add observed speed samples. Five qualifying recent samples replace the static speed input on later selections. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-store/src/lib.rs:2008-2020`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-store/src/lib.rs:2099-2136`)
12. On restart, the store migrations and open path restore durable facts, cursors, backoff, usage, and decisions; background Fusiform, AA, and quota consumers refresh external state. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-store/src/lib.rs:143-282`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-store/src/lib.rs:558-579`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-module/src/main.rs:113-123`)

## Failure modes and recovery
- Catalog transport failure leaves the prior cursor and facts untouched and retries; malformed prices become unknown cost and log once rather than aborting the sync. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-module/src/fusiform_catalog.rs:172-219`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-module/src/fusiform_catalog.rs:588-614`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-module/src/fusiform_catalog.rs:616-654`)
- AA rows that lack an exact configured map remain unscored; inline credentials are rejected in favor of a vault reference. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-module/src/aa_ingest.rs:27-110`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-module/src/config_overrides.rs:174-183`)
- Quota refresh runs every 60 seconds and retries after three seconds. Degraded failures keep the last healthy window, while `credential_absent`, `credential_unusable`, and `no_quota_reported` clear stale authority. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-module/src/main.rs:116-202`)
- Rolling quota windows are refused by pace projection; reported exhausted pools wall, while derived pool balances only affect pressure. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/quota.rs:114-125`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-core/tests/quota_overlay.rs:133-161`)
- Backoff considers only `error`, `session_error`, and `launch_failed`. Local-network, pre-dispatch, and content-policy attribution leave the streak unchanged, and `completed` resets it. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/failure_backoff.rs:37-61`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/failure_backoff.rs:63-182`)
- Under the default threshold, the third consecutive attributable failure begins a ten-minute backoff that doubles to six hours; `route.clear_failure_backoff` provides explicit operator recovery without changing cooldown or quota state. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/failure_backoff.rs:8-23`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/failure_backoff.rs:158-181`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-module/src/main.rs:3751-3762`)
- If all routes are inadequate, excluded, unavailable, quota-walled, or in backoff, selection returns a typed failure and still retains candidate rejection evidence for explanation. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/model_router.rs:655-776`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-module/src/main.rs:2684-2772`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-module/src/main.rs:3523-3593`)
- `route.explain` returns `not_found` with the 30-day retention statement when the lookup misses or the row was pruned. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-module/src/main.rs:3215-3230`)

## Open items
- The design base found that the fixed effectively-free bucket hides a 28-fold effective-price gap when adequacy saturates. The shipped scorer still ignores cost differences inside the 0.05 bucket. (`.cortexkit/alfonso/plans/routing-quota-economics-v1.md:5-14`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/routing_config.rs:82-82`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/model_router.rs:831-848`)
- Windowed-subscription slack is not part of the objective. The current objective remains normalized effective dollar cost plus quota pressure, while the design proposes pool classes, reset-time slack, and a ratio gate. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-core/src/model_router.rs:557-631`, `.cortexkit/alfonso/plans/routing-quota-economics-v1.md:20-31`)
- Observed speed and catalog speed still enter one tie-break even though observed samples use output tokens over duration and only replace catalog speed after enough samples. The design requires one decode-speed basis and treats unobserved catalog speed as unknown until then. (`crates/prefrontal-routing-store/src/lib.rs:2008-2020`, `crates/prefrontal-routing-store/src/lib.rs:2099-2136`, `.cortexkit/alfonso/plans/routing-quota-economics-v1.md:31-31`)
- UNVERIFIED: the current quota payload does not expose every design field needed for window slack, specifically `windowLengthMs` and a usable reset timestamp for every capacity key. The design names the required fields and says missing windows must decode as banked. (`.cortexkit/alfonso/plans/routing-quota-economics-v1.md:33-35`)
- The design leaves operator choices open for `k`, the cost-ratio threshold `R`, and whether pool base price is amortized plan price or current effective price. (`.cortexkit/alfonso/plans/routing-quota-economics-v1.md:48-48`)
